Control your Squeezebox Radio, Squeezebox Boom, Squeezebox Touch and other Squeezebox players from Homey, through Lyrion Music Server (formerly Logitech Media Server).

Features:
- Power, play/pause, next/previous track, volume and mute
- Now playing in the Homey media player: track, artist, album and album art
- Preset buttons 1–6 and a "Play preset" flow card
- Alarms: turn alarms on or off, see whether an alarm is ringing, snoozed or due, and when the next alarm goes off
- Bass, treble and display brightness buttons
- Flow triggers: track changed, alarm started, snoozed or ended
- Flow actions: play favorite, play preset, play URL/stream, sync and unsync players, turn alarms on or off

Lyrion servers on your network are found automatically when you add a player, and the app follows a server if its IP address changes. You can use several Lyrion servers at once, for example one for the Squeezebox Radio and another for the Squeezebox Touch. Lyrion Music Server is required.
